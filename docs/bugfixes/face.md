# Face / head bug log

Defects found in the head, face, eye, neck and portrait code while replacing the lofted-sphere head
(old code refers to `claude/world-realism-overhaul` @ 8860668). One line each: symptom -> cause -> fix.

1. Neck pokes through the jaw / gap opens at the nape when the head turns -> head parts were rigid `{"head": 1}` (characters.py:1616) while the separate neck used the chest/neck/head chain `w_neck` (characters.py:329), so the jaw underside and the neck top moved by different bones -> one continuous head+neck mesh with head/neck/chest weights blended along a jaw-to-skull-base cut line (face_rig.py:63 `neck_weights`).
2. A visible dark ring / air gap around every eye ("goggle" discs in realism_face.jpg) -> the eyelid was a separate open shell of radius 1.1x the eyeball (characters.py:477 `eyelid_shell(c, er * 1.1, ...)`), i.e. 1.2 mm off the globe, with an eyeball pushed 0.3 r behind the head surface (characters.py:470) -> lids are skin edge loops whose posterior margin sits at R + 0.15 mm and tucks behind the globe as a conjunctival fold (face_eyes.py:179-185 ring table, face_eyes.py:105 `lid_y`).
3. Eyes could not look anywhere -> eyeballs were weighted to `head` and the armature had no eye bones (characters.py:475) -> `eye.L` / `eye.R` bones at the eyeball centres, eyeballs weighted 100 % to them, lids follow gaze via `lid_look_up/down` (face_rig.py:30, face_anim.py `lids_follow`).
4. No blink or lid motion was possible and a hard material seam ran round each eye -> the lid shell was a separate mesh with its own `lid` material (characters.py:477) -> lids are part of the skin mesh (eye O-grid, face_mesh.py `_eye_ogrid`) with blink/squint/look/wide shape keys regenerated from the same lid geometry (face_shapes.py `lid_delta`).
5. Pinched shading and a triangle fan at the chin (and crown) -> the head was a UV sphere whose poles landed on the menton and vertex (characters.py:434) -> structured quad grid whose chin is an ordinary grid region, crown closed by a pole-free Coons patch (face_mesh.py:321 `_crown`).
6. Blurred / smeared texture on the chin, under the jaw and under the nose -> lat/long head UVs (`v = asin(z)`, characters.py:448) compress texels toward the chin pole and on surfaces the rays graze -> front-projected Lambert azimuthal face island plus cylindrical side island, re-spaced by measured surface arc length (face_uv.py:72 `_warps`).
7. Ears a different skin tone from the face, with texture seams across the pinna -> ears used the generic `skin` material and a box projection (characters.py:483-484) -> ears use the face material with their own UV boxes in the head texture (face_uv.py:210 `ear_uv`, face_head.py:65).
8. Colour / detail seam along the jaw line -> the neck used the 512 px `skin` material while the head used the face texture (characters.py:918-919) -> the neck is part of the head mesh and shares the face texture (characters.py `build_character` neck removed from the outfit; face_mesh.py rows run to the collar).
9. Characters could not open their mouths (talk / visemes impossible) -> the mouth was only painted on a closed surface (anatomy.py head_shape "lips" clay layers) -> mouth O-grid whose loops roll into a mouth bag, with teeth, gums and tongue that follow the jaw (face_mesh.py:459 `_mouth_ogrid`, face_mouth.py:74).
10. Flat, dark "painted" nostrils seen from below or in 3/4 view -> the nose had no openings, only dark texture blobs -> nostril O-grids with vestibule tunnels on the nose underside (face_mesh.py:485 `_nostril_ogrid`).
11. Dead, highlight-less eyes -> the eye was a single textured sphere with no cornea bulge (characters.py:472-475) -> eyeball with a recessed iris cone, limbal groove and a 7.8 mm corneal cap blended into a tear film (face_eyes.py:221 `eyeball`), refractive in Cycles portraits.
12. Head export took ~9 min per protagonist once the face had morph targets -> the glTF exporter re-evaluated every object at every sampled frame of all 136 actions to bake the shape-key channels -> `export_optimize_disable_viewport=True` so shape-key tracks are read from their F-curves (util.py:478).
13. Build crashed with "internal error setting the array" after merging the gait branch -> the gait baker keys every pose bone, including the new eye bones, so the face baker appended keys to non-empty F-curves -> clear the curve before writing (face_anim.py:378).
14. A sharp spike stuck out of the chin under the lower lip -> seen from the head centre the chin pad hides the mentolabial sulcus, so the outermost ray crossing jumped past it -> rays start 30 mm lower (`face_chart.ORIGIN`) and take the first exit from the inside (face_chart.py `cast`).
15. Flat, washed-out dialogue portraits -> the key light sat on the camera side (broad lighting), no depth of field, 256 px at 48 samples without supersampling (render_portraits.py:43-47, old) -> short lighting from the far side, catchlight fill, f/2.2 focus on the near eye, 2x render filtered down (render_portraits.py:138-156, preview.py:74).
16. Portraits caught characters mid-blink / mid-viseme -> the portrait posed the idle clip's frame 1, which now carries face animation -> the portrait mutes the face NLA tracks and sets a designed expression (render_portraits.py `pose`).
17. Hair and beards were placed against an analytic head (`characters.head_shape`, the old clay function) that did not match the built mesh; after the hair groom switched to ray-casting the head, the new lash and brow cards would have lifted the scalp -> the groom ray-casts a skin/eyeball/ear proxy of the built head without cards (face_head.py `scalp_proxy`, characters.py `build_character`); the analytic `head_shape` is gone.
18. Smile / sneer / look shape keys moved the two halves of the face by different amounts, and the right nostril turned the wrong way -> the O-grid blend rings found their anchor/far sides by comparing planar x positions (unstable for a grid that is only symmetric to float precision), the right-side rectangles were chosen independently on the surface, and the nostril ellipse rotation was not mirrored -> anchor sides come from grid column indices, right rectangles are mirrored column indices of the left ones, the nostril rotation flips with the side (face_mesh.py:170 `_loop_params`, face_mesh.py:233 `mirror`, face_mesh.py:476); the new symmetry check (face_check.py `mirror_error`) went from 3.1 mm to 0.000 mm.
19. A dark 2 mm slit between the upper lid and the eyeball near the inner corner -> the lid ellipsoid was stretched medially toward the caruncle (a_med ~ 1.3 R) over the whole medial half, so the margin floated off the globe long before reaching the canthus -> the ellipsoid stays spherical over the globe and stretches only past 0.8 R (face_eyes.py:111); lid gap 2.3 mm -> 0.4 mm (face_check.py `lid_seal`).
20. The iris looked small and oddly ringed, and in Godot the eyeballs risked back-face culling -> the eye frame built by `_frame` is left-handed (r x u = -forward), so every sclera, iris, cornea and caruncle face was wound inward: Cycles refracted the cornea inside-out and the sclera's subsurface ran the wrong way -> faces are oriented explicitly after construction (face_eyes.py:222 `_orient`, sclera/cornea outward, iris and limbus wall forward); new check `face_check.eyes_outward` (face_check.py:149).
21. A black wedge in the inner corner of every eye -> the fissure extends ~4 mm medial of the globe's silhouette, where it looked straight into the back of the conjunctival tuck (folded 5 mm deep), and the caruncle sat over the sclera instead of in the angle -> the tuck folds back at most 1.8 mm medially and the caruncle spans exactly from the globe edge to the canthus, its lateral half behind the sclera (face_eyes.py:117, face_eyes.py:363 `caruncle`).
22. The wet tear-line strips were invisible in the engine and dull in Cycles -> the strip quads were wound away from the viewer -> reversed winding (face_eyes.py:424).
23. A hard-edged box around each eye (a rectangle of different shading from brow to cheek) -> the outer skin loops of the lids followed the lid ellipsoid past the globe rim and folded 5 mm back, up to 9 mm under the sculpted surface, and the O-grid rectangle's top row (quantised to ~5 mm rows) could sit below the outermost lid loop, so the blend loops overlapped and folded (178 deg dihedrals) -> skin loops stop at the rim depth, loops buried under the surface are eased up to it, and the rectangle is sized from the lid loop extents (face_eyes.py:117, face_mesh.py:395, face_mesh.py:247); new check `face_check.folds` (face_check.py:112).
24. Eye and mouth blend loops sat up to 2.6 mm inside the head -> the Laplacian relaxation moved them without re-projecting onto the surface -> Newton projection onto the implicit surface after every pass (face_mesh.py:540, face_surface.py:428 `project`).
25. Women's necks looked thick and the jaw line vanished into them from the front -> the neck column (half-width 49.8 mm) was wider than the mandible at the gonion (47.5 mm), so the neck, not the jaw, drew the lower face outline -> a slimmer upper neck for women that regains full width at the collar (face_surface.py:415).
26. The hair started ~25 mm down the forehead -> `hairline_table` is a fixed unit-sphere elevation (0.42 female / 0.50 male at the front) that ignored the sculpted head, whose trichion sits at 0.62-0.64 -> the front of the table is moved onto the built head's trichion, fading out toward the temples, for both the groom and the skin painter (characters.py:463 `hairline_fn`, face_head.py:119).
27. Lip tint bled onto the skin around the mouth -> the painter's lip masks were soft lat/lon Gaussians centred on landmarks -> exact masks from the sculpted vermilion borders, with a depth gradient for the gradient-lip tint (face_head.py:124 `paint_masks`, skin.py:147).
28. Painted eyeliner floated on the lid above the lashes -> it was placed on an approximate lid curve in the painter's lon/lat frame -> liner painted from the real lash-line loop, thicker laterally with a short wing (face_head.py:157 `_liner_masks`).
29. Brown irises rendered grey in the portraits -> the iris surface had a 0.5 specular at roughness 0.35, whose sheen from the soft key swamped the ~3 % albedo -> a matte iris (the gloss belongs to the cornea) (skin_eyes.py:187).
30. Lashes read as a faint brown haze, so the eyes looked small and undefined -> each 0.65 mm lash card carried 70 hair-thin strands (~0.007 mm), which average with the skin behind them at any distance -> 34 thicker strands per card, dense at the root (skin_eyes.py:215).
31. In portraits the eyes over-rotated and diverged once the head was turned toward the lens -> `eye_contact` measured the view direction in world space, but eye-bone rotations are relative to the posed head -> the direction is expressed in the head's rest frame (render_portraits.py:127).
32. The male hero's crown looked like stiff spikes -> the second combed layer used a 24 mm bulge and wispy strips, so cards stood off the skull with ragged tips -> a close 13 mm layer of dense strips in more, shallower sections (hair_styles.py:339).
