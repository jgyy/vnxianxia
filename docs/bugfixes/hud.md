# HUD / UI bug log

One line per genuine defect: symptom -> cause -> fix (file:line).

1. The UI never scaled with the window: at 1920x1080 / 2560x1440 every panel, bar and font stayed at its 720p pixel size (a tiny HUD in the corners), and at 1024x768 the 1280-wide layout was cropped -> `project.godot` had no stretch mode, so controls were laid out in raw window pixels -> `window/stretch/mode="canvas_items"`, `aspect="expand"` on the 1280x720 base (godot/project.godot:24-25); all HUD pieces now hang off screen edges through containers.
2. The key-hint line at the bottom left ran into the dialogue box (at 720p it sat flush against the box's bottom edge and underneath its left side) -> it was an always-visible label at a fixed offset -> the hints are keycap chips that hide while `game.dialogue.active` (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:179, :618).
3. "Chapter N Complete" banner and the next quest's story card were drawn on top of each other at every chapter end -> `quest_runner` calls `banner()` and `quest_card()` in the same frame; the banner (centre-170) overlapped the card (top 86, ~150 tall) -> banners, the volume title card and quest cards share one centre-stage queue and play one after another (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:554, :562).
4. At a volume's end the new volume's title card faded in over the still-visible quest card (title card delayed 5.5 s, card shown for 6 s + fades, both around y 60-250) -> independent tweens with no ordering -> same queue; a title card's delay now counts from when it was requested but it never starts before the stage is free (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:554).
5. A boss conversation played with the boss bar drawn across the dialogue box -> `quest_runner` shows the bar within 40 m but starts the pre-fight dialogue at 24 m without hiding it, and the bar sat at the bottom centre (y 630-680) inside the dialogue region -> the boss bar now lives under the compass at the top, with the quest card flowing below it (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:233).
6. Evicting a sixth toast logged "Tween ... target object freed" errors -> `toast()` called `free()` on the oldest toast while its tween still had property tweeners and a `queue_free` callback bound to it -> the toast's tween is stored in meta and killed before the node is removed; the stack is capped at 4 and toasts wrap at 330 px so a long recap can't push past the screen (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:504).
7. The quest tracker and the new-quest card never shrank: after a long objective or quest summary the panel stayed tall with empty space for the rest of the session -> free-floating PanelContainers keep their largest size (Godot only grows a Control to its minimum size) -> the tracker and card are container children (SHRINK_END / SHRINK_CENTER) so they are re-fitted on every change (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:198, :246).
8. Vitality/qi/boss/meditation bars moved in 1 % steps: small qi regeneration ticks were invisible and a hit of < 1 % max HP produced no damage flash -> the bars were `ProgressBar`s with the default `step = 0.01` on a 0..1 range, so values were rounded -> gauges are TextureProgressBars with `step = 0` and an eased display value (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:724).
9. Long prompts ("E  <interact text>") and long banner / title-card lines drifted right of centre -> fixed-width labels (600 / 1000 / 1200 px) with a hard-coded negative x offset grow only to the right when the text is wider -> the prompt is a shrink-centred plate sized by its content, banners/titles are centred in a container (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:315, :338).
10. Toasts were pinned at y=170, so any taller vitals panel (a two-line realm name, the new cultivation row) slid under them -> absolute position -> toasts flow in the same column directly below the vitals panel (godot/scripts/ui/hud.gd:169).
