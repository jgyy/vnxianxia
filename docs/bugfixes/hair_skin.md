# Hair + skin bug log

Genuine defects found in the hair / skin / material code while rebuilding it
(line numbers of the old code refer to commit 8860668).  Format: symptom -> cause -> fix.

1. Textures such as `tex.stone(..., 128)` came out as NaN (black/garbage in the GLB) -> `tex.fbm` skipped every octave when the base cell count exceeded half the texture size and then divided by `total == 0` (tex.py:53-59) -> the first octave always runs with its cell count clamped to size/2 (tex.py:51).
2. Dark smudge / broken shading on the crown of every haircap -> the scalp shell was closed with a single 72-vertex n-gon whose UVs were all (0.5, 1.2), so MikkTSpace tangents were undefined and the normal map shaded garbage there (characters.py:588-590) -> the new cap closes the crown with a triangle fan carrying real UVs (hair_cards.py:163).
3. "Plastic helmet" hair highlight -> hair was an isotropic GGX lobe (roughness 0.42-0.54, no tangent, no secondary lobe) on smooth tubes and a solidified shell (tex.py:482, characters.py:1105) -> alpha-tested strand cards with an anisotropic lobe along the UV tangent rotated by a flow map, a tinted sheen for the secondary (TRT-like) lobe and back-lit translucency (skin_shading.py:204, hair_tex.py:279).
4. Hard, raised rim where hair met the forehead -> the cap was solidified 4 mm and inflated 3.5-8 % right up to the hairline, so its edge stood proud of the skin (characters.py:578-592) -> a 1.5 mm cap set back 0.03 behind the hairline, with sparse wispy root-faded cards along the hairline (hair_cards.py:141, hair_styles.py `gathered`, hair_tex.py:201).
5. Hair streaks on buns / topknots ran in arbitrary axis-aligned directions with seams -> the knots were spheres UV-mapped with `util.box_uv` (characters.py:657, 689 via `solid`) -> buns are strand cards coiled around the knot axis (hair_groom.py:280).
6. Hero faces had blurrier roughness (T-zone and lip gloss) than NPC faces -> `util.material(detail_div=face_tex // 1024)` baked metal/roughness at 1/(2n) although its docstring says 1/n, so a 2048 hero face got a 512 map while a 1024 NPC face kept 1024 (util.py:59 vs 89, characters.py:1098) -> the skin material chooses its map sizes explicitly: albedo full, ORM and normal half (skin.py:448).
7. Face texture generation spent seconds on freckles and put some on lips, scalp and the back of the head -> 40 full-resolution `exp()` passes over the whole 2048^2 UV image with uniform UV placement, stretched by the face UV warp (tex.py:318-321) -> sparse moles/freckles placed in face coordinates, masked off the lips (skin.py:215).
8. Stair-stepped, aliased fine skin lines in the normal map -> the line pattern was thresholded into a binary step `(lines < 0.12) * 0.1` before becoming height (tex.py:294) -> smooth ridged noise lines stretched along skin tension directions (skin.py:186).
9. Sclera looked like flat white paint -> the vessel mask only started at 30-70 % of the sphere radius from the pupil (tex.py:445), i.e. entirely behind the eyelids -> vessels fade in just outside the limbus and thicken toward the canthi (skin_eyes.py:89).
10. Male beard shadow ended in a dark line along the jaw / neck seam -> the stubble mask `sstep(-0.45, -0.75, lat)` stayed at full strength down to the bottom of the head mesh, where the neck uses the separate body-skin material without stubble (tex.py:342) -> the shadow fades out under the jaw before the seam (skin.py:151).
11. Alpha-tested cards rendered as solid black clumps in Cycles portraits/previews -> `preview.setup` left Cycles at its default 8 transparent bounces, fewer than the layers of hair cards (preview.py:14) -> 64 transparent bounces (preview.py:15).
