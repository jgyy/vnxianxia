# Story pipeline: bugs found and fixed

Genuine defects in the story data and its pipeline (tools/story, tools/build_story.py, tools/gen_voices.py), found
during the group-conversation / enlarged-world overhaul. One line each: symptom -> cause -> fix.

1. docs/QUESTS.md listed only 1000 of the 2000 quests (the first half of every chapter) under a "1000 quests" heading -> `quest_table` sliced `c["first"]:c["first"] + 10` from the old 10-quest chapters -> slice by `NB.QUESTS_PER_CHAPTER` and count the quests in the heading (tools/build_story.py:1033).
2. Elder Gu, still the trusted Law Hall elder in chapter one, answered the player's choices like a prisoner ("Kinder than I was, at your age", "That is how it started, for me"), giving the traitor away 200 quests early -> `CATEGORY[GU] = "prisoner"` overrode his elder temperament for the whole saga -> `category(nid, q)` is time-aware: elder until he is unmasked (tools/story/morality.py:60).
3. Social quests with Gu before his unmasking offered rogue dilemmas ("still carries a blade taken in the old days") and he never greeted the player -> choice families and greetings read the same unconditional "prisoner" category -> `choice_family`/`infer_family`/`greeting_lines` pass the quest number (tools/story/saga_gen.py:1277, tools/story/morality.py:359).
4. The imprisoned Gu shouted generic battle cries in fights after his arrest ("Weapons up! This is going to get loud.", q0834, q1653) -> the fight ally was chosen by character model only, and Gu uses the ordinary elder model -> the ally must not be demonic or a prisoner by temperament at that quest (tools/story/saga_gen.py:911).
5. Purely social conversations opened with after-battle lines ("You fight like the mountain taught you personally", "That's the last of it here") although nothing had happened -> every non-giver filler talk used the REGROUP pool -> a talk right after another talk opens with a MEET line and a fitting reply (tools/story/saga_gen.py:947, tools/story/fillers.py MEET).
6. After-action conversations ended with the protagonist promising to go and do the task ("I'll report back when it's done", "On my way") at the end of the quest -> the after-action filler reused the pre-task REPLY pool -> AFTER_REPLY for talks after an action, and the closing line of a group conversation is chosen by scene (tools/story/saga_gen.py:953, tools/story/saga_gen.py:1156).
7. "We're done at here.", "Catch your breath; here isn't done with us yet." (41 times each) and "Here is clear. For now." (47 times) -> REGROUP lines take a `{place}` that was the literal word "here" when the ally stood at home -> the home marker's phrase is used (tools/story/saga_gen.py:945).
8. Eight greeting lines could never be seen (juniors bowing to a Nascent Soul in quests where the player is at most Core Formation, q0142, q0234 ...) -> greetings were drawn without regard to the realm reachable at that quest -> `morality.can_hold` filters them, and build_story now rejects any line conditioned on a realm the player cannot have during its quest (tools/story/morality.py:347, tools/build_story.py:825).
9. Stage directions misgendered male speakers ("(starts to kneel, then catches themself)" said by Merchant Jin, "(bows so deeply they nearly topple)") -> category-wide greetings used a pronoun for the speaker -> pronoun-free directions (tools/story/morality.py:300, tools/story/morality.py:309).
10. The orphan protagonist was told "Stay close! I am not writing to your family." in battle cries -> filler ignored the premise (no family but a dead mother) -> the ally threatens to explain the death to the Sect Master instead (tools/story/fillers.py:117).
11. Choice reactions repeated up to 404 times across the saga ("Rules, rules. Fine. You're right. Again.") -> each temperament had two or three reactions per direction for ~6000 options -> reactions in the voice of the 16 most-met characters plus more lines per temperament; the most repeated reaction now appears ~140 times (tools/story/morality.py:183).
12. A moral choice made at a reach or interact objective was met with silence: nobody answered the player -> `attach_choice` added a reaction only from a talk objective's `npc` -> companions present at the objective react (and add asides after a talk) (tools/story/saga_gen.py:1392).
13. NPCs spoke in objectives they were not part of (Wei Tong's battle cries in fights, Han Xue commenting on arrivals), so the runtime had nobody to stand there or animate -> dialogue speakers were never tied to the objective -> objectives carry `with` (filled from the speakers by `dsl.fill_with`), and build_story rejects a speaker who is neither the `npc` nor in `with` (tools/story/dsl.py:129, tools/build_story.py:389).
14. "Read the Butcher's ledger", "Search the guild's salt tallies", "Study Iron-Fang's old fortress map" and five more placed a jade slip in the world -> `PROP_WORDS` mapped ledger/tallies/map to `jade_slip`, and any "altar" to the Blood Moon altar -> map-aware inference with the new props (abacus_desk, map_table, offering_table ...) (tools/story/saga_gen.py:1442, tools/story/saga_gen.py:1469).
15. Arrival narration at the main hall said "Inside the main hall, the founder's tablet gleams" although the MainHall marker is the terrace before the doors -> text written against the wrong spot -> "Through the open doors of the main hall ..." (tools/story/places.py:43).
16. "Regroup with Constable Du" / "Check on ..." objectives (16 quests) named people who had not been in the fight they were regrouping after -> the "after" objective text only looked at the previous step's type -> "after" wording only for someone who met the player earlier in the quest or shouted in the fight (tools/story/saga_gen.py:819).
17. Lines could not be added to a voiced conversation without moving the voice keys of every later line (and re-synthesising them with new text) -> keys were numbered by position among unconditional lines -> `added(...)` lines are keyed after the objective's original lines (tools/build_story.py:367).
18. build_story, gen_voices, numbering and the story package still described a 1000-quest saga of 10 quests per chapter, and the NPC window error message asked for ids "q0001..q1000" -> stale docstrings after the saga was doubled -> updated to 2000 quests / 20 per chapter (tools/build_story.py:10).
19. Collect objectives could be given dialogue that the runtime never plays -> no validation -> build_story rejects dialogue on collect objectives, and `dsl.collect` no longer accepts lines (tools/build_story.py:362).
